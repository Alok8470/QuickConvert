Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026 • Operated by Alok Maurya (support@quickconvert.in)

1. In-Browser File Processing (Your Files Never Leave Your Device)

The fundamental architectural feature of QuickConvert is local processing. When you use tools such as Images to PDF, Image Compressor, PDF Merge, Split, Rotate, Watermark, or Sign:

Files Remain on Your Local Hardware

Your documents, images, and texts are processed purely within your web browser’s memory using JavaScript and WebAssembly libraries (such as pdf-lib, pdf.js, and jsPDF). Your file data is never uploaded to our servers, streamed across external networks, or stored in any remote database. Once you close or reload the browser tab, the temporary working memory holding your file is cleared by your browser.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Cookies (Google AdSense)

QuickConvert is free to use and funded by web advertising. We partner with Google AdSense to display advertisements across our pages.

  • Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to QuickConvert and/or other sites across the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Web Analytics & Tag Manager

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to monitor aggregated website metrics—such as page load speeds, popular tools, referring websites, and general browser/operating system types.

This telemetry does not track individual identity or inspect files processed by our tools. Analytics data is aggregated and used solely to diagnose technical issues and improve website performance.

4. Contact & Feedback Submissions

If you voluntarily submit our Contact form (contact.html) or User Feedback form (feedback.html), we collect the details you provide (your name, email address, star rating, and message text).

This information is processed through form services (such as Formspree) and delivered to our operator email (support@quickconvert.in). We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and evaluate feature requests. We never sell, rent, or distribute your email address to marketers.

5. Server Logs & Hosting

Our web host (e.g., Vercel / Cloudflare CDN) automatically logs standard web server metadata when delivering static website files (HTML, CSS, JS, SVG). This includes your IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, and timestamps. These logs are maintained by the hosting provider for network security, DDoS mitigation, and uptime monitoring.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Inquiries

Because we do not store your converted files or account profiles, there is no file database to delete. However, if you have submitted a message or feedback and wish to have your submitted correspondence deleted from our email records, please email:
