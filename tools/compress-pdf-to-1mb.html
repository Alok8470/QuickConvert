How to Compress a PDF Online — Step-by-Step Guide

Reduce scanned marksheets, Aadhaar cards, and document PDFs to exact KB requirements.

1

Select PDF File

Drag and drop your PDF into the upload zone or choose from your device. QuickConvert loads the document directly into browser memory.

2

Set Target KB

Choose an automated preset (100KB, 200KB, 500KB, 1MB) or enter your exact portal requirement in the target box.

3

Click Compress

The client-side engine renders each page canvas, strips bloated streams, and packs a newly compressed PDF stream in milliseconds.

4

Instant Download

Review the savings percentage and click Download to save the optimized PDF file directly to your downloads folder.
