What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

When goods or services are sold within the same state (intra-state), GST is equally divided into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). When goods are sold across state borders (inter-state), Integrated GST (IGST) is charged entirely to the Central Government.

Can I calculate custom GST percentages?

Yes! Simply type any rate into the Custom % box (e.g., 0.25%, 3% for gold and precious stones, or 15%) and the tool updates the tax breakdown instantly.

Is this GST Calculator accurate for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filings?

Yes. The tool follows official GST Council rounding rules (two decimal places, half-even rounding) suitable for invoices, books of accounts, and official GSTR returns.
