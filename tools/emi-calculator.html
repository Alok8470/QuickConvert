Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to loan EMI and repayment calculations

What is Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)?

EMI is a fixed monthly payment made by a borrower to a lender on a specified date every month. Each payment includes both principal repayment and interest charges, structured so the entire loan balance reaches zero at the end of the tenure.

Why is interest higher during the early years of a loan?

Because interest is calculated on the remaining outstanding principal. During early years, the outstanding balance is high, so the majority of your EMI goes toward interest. As the principal drops, interest decreases and more of each payment pays down the principal.

How does loan tenure affect my monthly EMI?

A longer tenure lowers your monthly payment because repayment is spread over more months. However, you pay significantly more total interest over the life of the loan. A shorter tenure increases monthly EMI but minimizes overall interest cost.

Are processing fees and insurance included in this EMI?

This calculator computes pure loan interest and principal amortization. Bank processing fees (typically 0.5% - 2%) and mandatory insurance premiums are separate one-time upfront costs charged at loan disbursement.
