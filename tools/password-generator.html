Why Browser Cryptography Matters

Unlike cloud-hosted password generators that create credentials on remote servers, QuickConvert generates every character locally utilizing your browser's cryptographically secure pseudo-random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues). Passwords are never sent over the internet or logged in any database.
