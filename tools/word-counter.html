Why Word Count & Reading Time Matters

Content length is directly correlated with search engine readability and social engagement. Standard blog posts typically target 1,200 to 2,000 words for comprehensive topical coverage. Social platforms enforce hard limits (280 characters for X/Twitter, 2,200 for Instagram captions, 3,000 for LinkedIn posts).
