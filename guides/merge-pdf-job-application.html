How to Merge PDFs for Job Applications

Combine your resume, cover letter, university marksheets, experience letters, and ID documents into a single professional PDF portfolio.

Why Combine Job Documents into One File?

Modern applicant tracking systems (such as Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and Indian recruitment portals like TCS iON, Infosys Launchpad, and government job portals) frequently provide only a single document upload slot labeled "Attach Resume / Supporting Documents".

Submitting a single well-structured PDF portfolio ensures that hiring managers review your resume and supporting credentials together without missing attached emails or secondary links.

Recommended Document Order

Recruiters spend an average of 7 to 10 seconds on initial scans. Structure your combined PDF logically in this sequence:

1 Cover Letter (Tailored to the role)
2 Curriculum Vitae / Resume (1 to 2 pages maximum)
3 Highest Degree Certificate & Consolidated Marksheets
4 Experience Letters / Relieving Letters / Internship Proof
5 Government ID (Aadhaar / Passport / PAN) & Professional Certifications

Handling Size Limits (Under 2MB or 5MB)

Most corporate and government job portals enforce strict total file upload size limits (typically 2MB or 5MB). When compiling several scanned certificates, file sizes can quickly balloon. Here is how to keep your application PDF compact:

  • Export your resume directly from Word or Google Docs as PDF rather than printing and re-scanning it. Digital PDFs weigh under 100 KB.
  • For certificates and marksheets, scan at 150 DPI in grayscale if color is not strictly needed.
  • Use QuickConvert Merge PDF to combine all pages locally in your browser without exposing your confidential employment and salary history to third-party web servers.
