Passport Size Photo Sheet Printing Guide

How to format standard 3.5cm x 4.5cm photos and arrange 6, 8, or 12 copies on a printable sheet to print at home or at a local photo studio.

Official Indian Passport Photo Specifications

Whether you are applying for an Indian Passport via Passport Seva Kendra, an OCI card, a driving license, a PAN card, or a visa, authorities require photos meeting exact biometric standards:

  • Dimensions: Exactly 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (35 mm × 45 mm).
  • Print Resolution: 300 DPI (translates to 413 × 531 pixels).
  • Face Coverage: Face must occupy 70% to 80% of the frame (measured from crown of head to chin).
  • Background: Plain white or off-white. No patterned backgrounds, shadows, or reflections on eyeglasses.

Standard Photo Print Sheet Sizes

Printing multiple photos on a single photo sheet costs a fraction of studio rates. Standard photo paper sizes include:

4 × 6 Inch Photo Sheet

Holds 6 to 8 passport photos (arranged in a 2 × 3 or 2 × 4 grid with cutting margins). Most popular and cheapest size to print at local digital studios.

A4 Standard Sheet

Holds 24 to 32 passport photos. Ideal for printing on color inkjet or laser photo printers when multiple copies are needed for family applications.

Step-by-Step: Prepare Your Sheet

  1. Capture with Phone: Stand 4 feet from a white wall under even natural lighting. Have someone take a front-facing portrait.
  2. Crop to 3.5 × 4.5 Ratio: Crop your photo ensuring the head and upper shoulders are centered.
  3. Compile to PDF Sheet: Using QuickConvert Image to PDF, select A4 or 4x6 page dimensions, arrange multiple copies of the cropped photo, and download the print-ready PDF file.
  4. Print at Studio: Transfer the file to a USB flash drive or phone and print on glossy photo paper for a few rupees.
